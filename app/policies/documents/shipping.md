## Lost shipments

A shipment is treated as lost when it has been in transit for 10 days or more without being delivered. The customer may choose a full refund or a replacement shipment. A package still in transit for fewer than 10 days is not yet lost; ask the customer to wait and check the tracking again later.

## Delivered but not received

When tracking shows an order as delivered but the customer says the package never arrived, the case cannot be resolved automatically. Escalate it to a human for a carrier investigation.

## Replacement shipments

A free replacement is available for damaged items reported within 30 days of delivery and for lost shipments. Only one replacement is sent per order. Replacements for orders worth more than 150.00 USD must be approved by a team lead. An order that was refunded cannot also be replaced.

## Duplicate charges

If payment records show that a customer was charged more than once for the same order, the duplicate amount is refunded. Only the extra charge is refunded, not the order itself. If the payment records show a single charge, there is no duplicate to refund; explain this to the customer and suggest they check for a pending authorisation with their bank.

## Billing disputes

Disputes about pricing, promotions, taxes or subscription charges that are not a duplicate charge are not covered by an automatic rule. Request human approval or escalate.

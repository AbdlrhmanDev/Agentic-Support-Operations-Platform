## Refund window

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Requests made after the 30-day window are not eligible for an automatic refund. An order that has not been delivered cannot be refunded as a return.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or defective and the customer reports it within 30 days of delivery, they are entitled to a full refund of the item price or a free replacement, whichever they prefer. Do not offer both a refund and a replacement for the same order.

## Change of mind returns

A customer who simply no longer wants an item may return it for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After 30 days, change of mind returns are refused.

## Refund amounts and approval

A refund can never exceed what the customer paid for the order, less anything already refunded. Refunds up to 100.00 USD are issued automatically. Refunds above 100.00 USD must be approved by a team lead before they are issued. Refunds above 1000.00 USD are never issued by the support agent and must be escalated to a human.

## Orders already refunded or cancelled

An order that was already refunded in full cannot be refunded again. Cancelled orders are not charged and are not eligible for a refund.
